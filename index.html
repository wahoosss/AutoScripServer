Privacy Policy

Effective: September 2026 | Product: AutoScrip (this app) | This app requires a network connection

AutoScrip is an on-device UI automation tool. It is offered free of charge and financed by advertising: the app connects to the network to load ads and to report the minimal diagnostics needed to keep it running. Please read this policy before use; continuing to use the app means you accept it.

1. What we collect and why

1.1 Script data: stays on your device

The taps and swipes you mark, their coordinates, durations, start delays, loop counts, plus the script name and description you type yourself, are written to the app-private storage of your device. They are never uploaded and are never used to target ads. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

1.2 Data required to serve ads and keep the app working

To load ads, measure feature availability and diagnose crashes, we collect:

  • Device and app information: model, brand, OS version, screen resolution and density, app version, language and region;
  • Network information: connection type (Wi-Fi / cellular), coarse region derived from the IP address (the full IP is not stored);
  • Advertising identifier: the device advertising identifier used for ad delivery and attribution. You can reset or disable it in system settings;
  • Usage and diagnostic information: how often features are used, success/failure result codes of script runs, crash stacks and where they happened.

None of the above includes your contacts, SMS, call logs, photo library or precise location, and it never includes passwords or payment details you type into other apps.

1.3 Advertising

Ads shown in the app are provided and placed by third-party ad platforms. For frequency capping, attribution and fraud detection, the ad SDK processes the device and network data listed in 1.2 under its own privacy policy. Ad content does not read your scripts or coordinates.

1.4 How third-party SDKs are disclosed

Every advertising or analytics SDK integrated into this app is listed on this page — name, data scope and a link to its official privacy policy — before it ships in a release. A third party that does not appear on this page is not running inside this app. List updates are published together with the app version.

2. The accessibility permission and what it can see

AutoScrip needs the system Accessibility service permission in order to read widget labels and to perform taps and swipes at the positions you set. This permission:

  • is granted by you in system settings and can be revoked there at any time; once it is off, all automation stops immediately;
  • is used only to run the scripts you composed yourself; the app does not browse, record or upload the content of other apps on its own;
  • is not used to recognise, log or upload sensitive content such as passwords or payment information that you enter in other apps.

Recommendation: do not run automation on pages involving payments, banking or identity verification. Script coordinates are stored on your device — keep that in mind when you share the device or restore a backup.

3. Storage, sharing and protection

  • Where and how long: on-device data lives in app-private storage; data reported over the network is kept on the server for at most 12 months, then deleted or anonymised;
  • Transport security: all network requests use HTTPS, and ad or analytics requests never carry your script content;
  • Scope of sharing: we share data with third parties only as far as advertising, analytics and troubleshooting require, and we do not sell your personal information. We may disclose data where the law compels us to, and we will notify you where that is permitted;
  • Cross-border transfers: if a third-party service involves transfers outside your region, we will state it separately on this page when it is integrated and ask for your consent.

4. Your controls

  • Delete individual scripts inside the app, or clear all local data via “Settings → Apps → Storage → Clear data”;
  • Revoke the accessibility permission at any time in system settings;
  • Reset or disable the device advertising identifier to opt out of interest-based ads (you will still see ads, just not personalised ones);
  • Blocking network access for this app in system settings prevents ads from loading and diagnostics from being reported; your local scripts keep working.

5. Minors

This app is intended for adult users. If a minor uses it, do so with the knowledge and consent of a guardian. We do not run personalised advertising aimed at minors.

6. Changes to this policy

When functionality or data processing changes, we update this page and show the latest version in the app. Anything that adds data collection, adds a third party or changes a purpose will be highlighted before it takes effect.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy, or to exercise your rights of access, correction and deletion, email the developer at wallehu99@gmail.com, and include the app version (see “Settings → Version”).
